Section 1: Background



"What course are you on, and what year are you in?"

"How often do you practise coding on your own outside of lectures?"

"Can you walk me through the last time you sat down to code on your own?"

Probe: "What were you working on, and how did it go? "



Section 2: Progress and self-doubt



"How do you currently know whether you're improving at coding?"

Probe: "Do you track it in any way, or is it more of a feeling?"

"Have you ever felt behind, or like you weren't as good as you should be at this stage?"

If yes: "Can you tell me about a time that happened?"

If no: "What do you think helps you avoid feeling that way?"

"What was going on around that time?"

Probe: "What do you think triggered it?"

"What, if anything, helped you get through it?"



Section 3: Motivation and other people



"What keeps you motivated when you're coding alone, and what makes you lose motivation?"

"Have you ever coded or studied with other people? How was that different from doing it alone?"

"How do you feel about seeing how your classmates or peers are getting on with their coding?"

Follow up whichever way they answer: "Why do you think that is?"



Section 4: Existing tools



"Do you use any apps or tools to track or motivate your coding practice?"

Probe: "What do you like or dislike about them?"

"Have you tried anything and stopped using it? Why?"



Wrap-up



"If you could change one thing about how you learn to code alone, what would it be?"

"Is there anything else you think I should know that I haven't asked?"
